0124 Lakeside House · Dalarna · 118 m²

The slab is the only thing we pour. Everything above it arrives finished, on three trailers, in the order it goes together.

1.1Cladding 1.2Structure 1.3Openings

Untreated pine silvers in three winters. After that we never touch it.

1.1 Cladding Heartwood pine, 26 mm, vertical, open joint. No stain, no coating, no maintenance cycle.
1.2 Structure Cross-laminated spruce, cut to the millimetre in Trondheim, carrying 2 kN/m² of snow.
1.3 Openings Triple glazing in dark anodised aluminium, U 0.7, tested at −35 °C before it leaves the hall.
1.4 Services Balanced ventilation with 85 % heat recovery, ducted inside the floor cassette.
